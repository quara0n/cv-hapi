The `cv review` row showed a −100% three-month change and 0% year-over-year change; `resume review` showed −100% in both columns. Small ranges and rounded changes make these weak trend signals. `ai cv review` showed +∞ in both columns, which does not establish a large absolute market. Missing data is not proof of zero demand.

The review-focused English searches exist, but their reported demand is small. Do not sum close variants or assume all searchers intend to pay. The checker estimate suggests competition can readily exceed the product's illustrative break-even CPC; do not chase top placement at the historical upper bid. There is no evidence to justify scaling spend, broadening to generic CV traffic merely to exhaust the budget, or committing recurring ad spend.

Recommendation: retain the strict NOK 300 total Search learning ceiling, use exact/phrase review terms with honest £2 copy, and accept that delivery may be limited. Use the Macedonian video organically meanwhile. A separate Meta test needs a separate budget and a retained-sale measurement plan. Do not split NOK 300 between platforms and call it a meaningful creative comparison.
