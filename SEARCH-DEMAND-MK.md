## Implications

There is measurable CV-related demand, especially broad CV and Europass queries. Dedicated generic builder queries are much smaller. These figures do not establish an underserved market or willingness to pay. Ads competition is high for cv maker and cv template. Do not treat all Europass searchers as prospective buyers for an unrelated builder.

Keep the NOK 300 pilot small. At a hypothetical 5% purchase conversion and NOK 10 price, gross break-even CPC is NOK 0.50 before processing fees, taxes and operating costs. Thirty sales cover NOK 300 of ads in gross revenue only. The current free pilot measures engagement, not payment conversion; paid demand remains untested.

Macedonian is absent from Google's supported ad languages. English ads directed to the English page in North Macedonia test only a subset of the market. Organic Macedonian pages remain useful. Policy source: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6333734?hl=en
